A COMPANION, NOT A SCRIPT

Start with curiosity.

Sponsor Deck is a collection of 72 original conversation cards for sponsors and sponsees. Use them together, reflect on your own, or return to a question later.

You can pass on any question. Share only what you choose. These prompts support conversation; they do not replace your own program, trusted guidance, professional care, or emergency support.

Independent by design

This tool is not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous or AA World Services. No AA literature or official Step text is reproduced. Step cards are original discussion prompts, not instructions for working the Steps.

Privacy in plain language

The app has no analytics, accounts, ads, or external fonts. It makes no requests to send your notes or favorites anywhere. Your hosting provider may log ordinary page requests. Saving notes is optional; saved data stays in this browser until you delete it or the browser clears it.
