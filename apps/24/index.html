Keep them on your phone

Add each app to your Home Screen for quick access. On iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open an app in Safari

    Choose Rent Tracker or Meeting Schedule above.

  2. Tap Share

    Open the browser’s Share card. Share may be inside the page menu or the ••• menu.

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the Share card and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. If shown, keep Open as Web App on.

Repeat for the other app to get two separate icons on your Home Screen.

Using an Android phone?

Open either app in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then choose Add to Home screen or Install app. Follow the prompts, then repeat for the other app.
