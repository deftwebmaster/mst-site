StreetBond, manufactured by GAF, an advanced coating for either flat or stamped asphalt surfaces and concrete with proven performance since 1992 in over 40 countries worldwide. StreetBond coatings are specially formulated to:

  • Protect asphalt and concrete against UV, water, and chemical damage 
  • Provide superior slip and skid resistance making StreetBond coated surfaces safer for pedestrian and vehicle traffic
  • Designed to conform to normal expansion and contraction of pavement surfaces and will not peel, delaminate, shrink, or crack 
  • Color stability with UV stable, fade-resistant pigments
  • Keep pavement and environments cooler with solar reflective colors
  • Renews existing asphalt or concrete surfaces

Crosswalks – Sidewalks – Streetscapes – Parks – Plazas – Entryways – Schools – Cycle/Bus Lanes

StreetBond (asphalt and concrete) advanced coatings adhere permanently to pavement surfaces with unlimited creative design for hardscape and streetscape projects producing artistic beautiful surfaces. StreetBond works in almost any condition and can be used to color and protect both flat as well as stamped asphalt surfaces. Independent lab reports show StreetBond has superior durability, color fastness, adhesion, chemical resistance, and skid resistance.

StreetBondSR (asphalt and concrete) is offered in solar reflective colors to minimize Urban Heat Island Effect by reducing the amount of sun absorbed by pavement and provide LEED credits. The solar reflective colors reflect some of the sun’s heat making asphalt or concrete cooler.
