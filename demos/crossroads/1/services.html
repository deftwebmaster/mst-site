Services & products

Decorative pavement, start to finish.

Innovative design, cutting-edge materials and expert installation to transform asphalt and concrete — for cities, campuses, developers and landscape architects.

Stamped asphalt

The look of brick and stone, built into the street.

We heat existing or new asphalt, press in a brick, herringbone, cobblestone or custom pattern, and finish it with StreetBond color. The result looks like pavers but stays one continuous, drivable surface.

  • Brick, herringbone, cobblestone and custom patterns
  • Works on new or existing asphalt
  • Ideal for main streets, crosswalks, roundabouts, medians and plazas

StreetBond® coatings

Advanced color coatings for asphalt and concrete.

StreetBond, manufactured by GAF, is an advanced coating for flat or stamped asphalt and concrete, with proven performance since 1992 in over 40 countries. CrossRoads is a certified StreetBond applicator. StreetBond coatings are formulated to:

  • Protect asphalt and concrete against UV, water and chemical damage
  • Provide superior slip and skid resistance for pedestrian and vehicle traffic
  • Flex with the pavement’s normal expansion and contraction — no peeling, delaminating, shrinking or cracking
  • Hold their color with UV-stable, fade-resistant pigments
  • Renew the look of existing asphalt or concrete
See StreetBond projects

StreetBondSR · Cool pavement

Cooler surfaces with solar-reflective color.

StreetBondSR comes in solar-reflective colors that reflect some of the sun’s heat, keeping asphalt and concrete cooler. That helps reduce the urban heat island effect, makes public spaces more comfortable, and can contribute to LEED credits.

  • Solar-reflective colors for asphalt and concrete
  • Helps reduce urban heat island effect
  • Can contribute to LEED credits
