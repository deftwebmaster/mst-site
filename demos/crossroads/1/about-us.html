About CrossRoads

Built on craft, service and a lot of asphalt.

Paul Black entered the decorative asphalt industry in 1998 and started CrossRoads Decorative Pavement in January 2005. Paul trained in Canada, where these products and techniques were originally developed.

For more than two decades, CrossRoads has been at the forefront of the industry — playing a major role in the development of decorative pavement technology by testing new products and machinery and developing new application techniques.

The company is founded on superior, client-oriented customer service, dependability, integrity and dedication to quality. We specialize in street beautification for neighborhood enhancement and revitalization, decorative pedestrian plazas, safer crosswalks and trails, and custom logos that build community pride.
